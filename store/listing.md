## Full description (both stores, max 4000 chars)
Deep in the jungle, a stone frog idol guards a carved board of glowing gems.
Swipe to slide the gems, merge matching ones and work your way up to the legendary 2048 gem.

Temple 2048 is the classic 2048 puzzle you already know, rebuilt with the look and feel of a jungle temple adventure: glossy bevelled gems, a carved stone board with golden trim, flickering torches, drifting fireflies and a frog idol that reacts to every merge.

HOW TO PLAY
• Swipe up, down, left or right to slide all gems.
• Two gems with the same number merge into one when they touch.
• Reach 2048 to forge the sacred gem, then keep going for an even higher score.
• Stuck? Spend a booster: undo a swipe, smash a gem with the hammer, or shuffle the board.

DAILY TRIAL
• Every day a new board, the same for every player, with three goals to earn stars.
• Keep your streak alive by playing a little each day and fill the calendar with stars.

FIVE WAYS TO PLAY
• Classic – the 4 x 4 quest for the 2048 gem.
• Time Attack – two minutes, score as much as you can.
• Zen Garden – no game over, the temple makes room when you are stuck.
• Mini 3 x 3 – tiny board, brutal choices.
• Grand 5 x 5 – room to breathe, forge the 4096 gem.

YOUR TEMPLE
• A gem gallery that remembers every gem you forged for the first time.
• Chronicles with your lifetime stats: games, merges, best combo, streaks and stars.
• Share your score or your temple with friends.

FEATURES
• Satisfying merge effects: bursts, combo bonuses, screen shake and a frog idol that reacts.
• Ambient temple music and a heartbeat when the board is almost full.
• Every mode saves automatically, so you can pick up where you left off.
• Works completely offline.
• No ads, no account, no data collection.
• Crisp graphics on every screen size.

A quick game for a coffee break, or a long session chasing your best score. How far into the temple can you get?
