## Full description (both stores, max 4000 chars)
Deep in the jungle, a stone frog idol guards a carved board of glowing gems.
Swipe to slide the gems, merge matching ones and work your way up to the legendary 2048 gem.

Temple 2048 is the classic 2048 puzzle you already know, rebuilt with the look and feel of a jungle temple adventure: glossy bevelled gems, a carved stone board with golden trim, flickering torches, drifting fireflies and a frog idol that reacts to every merge.

HOW TO PLAY
• Swipe up, down, left or right to slide all gems.
• Two gems with the same number merge into one when they touch.
• Reach 2048 to forge the sacred gem, then keep going for an even higher score.
• Out of moves? Undo your last swipe or start a new game.

FEATURES
• Satisfying merge effects: bursts, combo bonuses and screen shake on big merges.
• Undo button for one wrong swipe.
• Your board and best score are saved automatically, so you can pick up where you left off.
• Works completely offline.
• No ads, no account, no data collection.
• Crisp graphics on every screen size.

A quick game for a coffee break, or a long session chasing your best score. How far into the temple can you get?
