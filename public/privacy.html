Privacy Policy – Temple 2048

Last updated: 11 November 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the mobile game Temple 2048 (the "Game"), published by ShortsForge ("we", "us"), handles information. The short version: the Game does not collect, store or share any personal data.

Temple 2048 works fully offline. It has no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no server of ours that the Game talks to.

1. Information we collect

None. We do not collect names, email addresses, device identifiers, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information, and the Game never transmits data to us.

2. Data stored on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the Game saves a small amount of information in the app's local storage on your device only:

  • the current board, score and best score;
  • your sound (mute) preference;
  • how long you have played and whether you have already answered the "rate this game" message, so we do not ask again.

This data never leaves your device. Uninstalling the Game deletes it.

3. Third parties

PartyWhenWhat they receive
Apple App Store / Google PlayWhen you install the Game, or tap "Rate us" and the store page opensHandled by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies. We receive only anonymous, aggregated statistics (such as the number of downloads and crashes) that the stores show to every developer.

The Game contains no third-party SDKs for advertising, analytics or tracking.

4. Children

The Game is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the applicable age in your country). Because no data is collected, no parental consent mechanism is needed.

5. Permissions

The Game does not request any special device permissions. It may use the device's vibration (haptic) feature for feedback when tiles merge; this does not involve any data.

6. Future changes

If a future version of the Game adds features that involve data (for example online leaderboards, advertising or crash reporting), we will update this policy before releasing that version, explain exactly what is collected and why, and ask for any consent the law requires. The "Last updated" date above shows the current version.

7. Your rights

Since we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you believe this is not the case, or have any question about privacy in the Game, contact us and we will respond promptly.

8. Contact
